## Part A — Interspecies conversion basis (I4)

Human equivalent dose is derived by dividing the animal NOAEL, in mg/kg/day, by
the body-surface-area conversion factor for that species.

| Species | Conversion factor used by this sponsor |
|---|---|
| Mouse | 12.3 |
| Rat | 6.2 |
| Minipig | 4.2 |
| Dog | 1.8 |
| Cynomolgus monkey | 3.1 |

**Reference adult body weight assumed throughout: 60 kg.**

---

## Part B — Safety factor (I5)

The sponsor's stated default safety factor for a first-in-human starting dose is
**10**. A departure from the default requires a written justification recorded in
the dose-rationale section; application of the default requires none.

## Part E — Unit convention

Doses in the derivation chain are expressed in mg/kg. Total doses are expressed
in flat mg using the 60 kg reference body weight stated in Part A. Nonclinical
exposures are expressed in µg·h/mL and anticipated human exposures in ng·h/mL;
a comparison across the two is made after conversion.
