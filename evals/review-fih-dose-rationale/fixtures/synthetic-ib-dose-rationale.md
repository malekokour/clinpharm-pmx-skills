## 5.1 Mechanism

Ondaritinib is a selective agonist of the TR-4 receptor. Receptor engagement
produces downstream signalling in target tissue at concentrations achievable at
the doses proposed below.

## 5.2 Nonclinical safety inputs

Repeat-dose toxicity was characterised in the rat and the dog. NOAEL values are
taken from Toxicology Report TOX-ONR-001 v2.0 and Toxicology Report
TOX-ONR-002 v1.0.

| Species | NOAEL | Study |
|---|---|---|
| Rat | 60 mg/kg/day | TOX-ONR-001 |
| Dog | 15 mg/kg/day | TOX-ONR-002 |

## 5.3 Most-sensitive-species argument

The rat was carried forward as the most sensitive species on the basis of the
28-day repeat-dose findings, and the human equivalent dose derived from the rat
NOAEL was used for the starting-dose derivation.

Species human equivalent doses are compared in Table 5-4.

## 5.4 Human equivalent dose

Human equivalent doses were derived by division by the body-surface-area
conversion factor for each species, as stated in the sponsor's conversion basis
document.

| Species | NOAEL | Conversion factor | Human equivalent dose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rat | 60 mg/kg/day | 6.2 | 9.68 mg/kg |
| Dog | 15 mg/kg/day | 1.8 | 8.33 mg/kg |

## 5.5 Maximum recommended starting dose

Applying a safety factor of 10 to the rat human equivalent dose of 9.68 mg/kg
yields a maximum recommended starting dose of 1.20 mg/kg.

For a reference adult body weight of 60 kg this corresponds to a total dose of
72 mg.

## 5.6 Proposed starting dose

A starting dose of 5 mg is proposed. This is well below the maximum recommended
starting dose derived in section 5.5 and provides additional margin at the first
exposure in humans.

## 5.7 Escalation and maximum planned dose

Dose escalation proceeds under the schema stated in the clinical protocol. The
maximum planned dose of 120 mg is bounded by the anticipated exposure below.

| Quantity | Value |
|---|---|
| Anticipated human AUC0–24 at the maximum planned dose | 4,820 ng·h/mL |
| Bounding nonclinical exposure, dog NOAEL AUC0–24 | 18.6 µg·h/mL |
| Exposure margin at the maximum planned dose | 3.9-fold |
