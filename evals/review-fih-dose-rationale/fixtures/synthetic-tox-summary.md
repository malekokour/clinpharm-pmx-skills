## TOX-ONR-001 — 28-day repeat-dose toxicity study, rat (report v2.0)

Doses: 0, 15, 60 and 200 mg/kg/day by oral gavage.

**Findings.** At 200 mg/kg/day, decreased body-weight gain and reversible
hepatocellular hypertrophy. At 60 mg/kg/day and below, no adverse findings.

**NOAEL: 60 mg/kg/day.**

Systemic exposure at the NOAEL: AUC0–24 = 41.2 µg·h/mL; Cmax = 4.86 µg/mL.

## TOX-ONR-002 — 28-day repeat-dose toxicity study, dog (report v2.0)

Doses: 0, 3, 10 and 30 mg/kg/day by oral capsule.

**Findings.** At 30 mg/kg/day, emesis, reduced food consumption and reversible
elevations in hepatic transaminases. At 15 mg/kg/day — a dose level added in an
interim cohort and reported in v2.0 of this report — reversible transaminase
elevation was observed in two of six animals and was considered adverse.
At 10 mg/kg/day and below, no adverse findings.

**NOAEL: 10 mg/kg/day.**

This NOAEL was revised downward from the value reported in v1.0 of this report,
following the interim cohort described above. Version 2.0 supersedes v1.0.

Systemic exposure at the NOAEL: AUC0–24 = 18.6 µg·h/mL; Cmax = 2.14 µg/mL.

## TOX-ONR-003 — 28-day repeat-dose toxicity study, minipig (report v1.0)

Doses: 0, 10, 25 and 75 mg/kg/day by oral gavage.

**Findings.** At 75 mg/kg/day, gastrointestinal intolerance. At 25 mg/kg/day and
below, no adverse findings.

**NOAEL: 25 mg/kg/day.**

Systemic exposure at the NOAEL: AUC0–24 = 29.4 µg·h/mL; Cmax = 3.31 µg/mL.
