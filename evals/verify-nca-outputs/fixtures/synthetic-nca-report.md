## 2 Methods

Plasma rembrolide concentration-time data were analysed by non-compartmental
methods.

AUC was calculated by the **linear-up/log-down trapezoidal method**.

**BLQ concentrations were set to zero throughout the profile.**

Terminal half-life was estimated by log-linear regression over at least three
points in the terminal phase.

Parameters were derived using **nominal sampling times**.

Reported values were taken from parameter dataset **RBL-102-PP-v1.0**.
