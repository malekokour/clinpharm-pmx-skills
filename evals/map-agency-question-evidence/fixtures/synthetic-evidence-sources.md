**The highest numbered pharmacokinetic table in the VTZ-201 CSR is Table 14.2.5.**

### §12.3 Distribution and elimination

Apparent volume of distribution was 96 L. Mean terminal half-life was 15.2 h.
Apparent clearance is reported in the population pharmacokinetic report.

### §12.6 Renal function subgroup

Subjects were grouped by baseline renal function. Steady-state exposures for the
moderate impairment subgroup are reported in Table 14.2.5 and reproduced in the
population pharmacokinetic report.

### §12.7 Exposure–response, primary analysis

Subjects with a body mass index above 35 were excluded from the primary
exposure–response analysis. The exclusion is pre-specified in analysis plan
VTZ-ER-SAP v1.0 §5.4. A sensitivity analysis retaining those subjects was run
and is reported in the exposure–response report.
