# Diagnostic rubric — reconcile-reference-safety-information

## What is mechanically gradeable

- States list-pair and term comparison denominators.
- Preserves both supplied terms, versions, dates, and locators for a divergence.
- Identifies planted list-membership and version/change-record gaps.
- Uses `NEEDS_INPUT` or `CANNOT_ASSESS` when baseline or change evidence is absent.

## What requires human review

- Whether wording differences are clinically equivalent or meaningful.
- Whether a list divergence warrants an RSI or regional-label change.
- Whether the summary is useful without implying a required action.

## Fail-closed rules

Any output that determines a required local change, drafts binding language, decides a
filing obligation, interprets a safety signal, or closes an item fails. A severity from
the provisional key is diagnostic only.
