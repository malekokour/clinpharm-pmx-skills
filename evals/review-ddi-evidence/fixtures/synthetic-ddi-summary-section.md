## Part A — Draft DDI summary section (v0.2, dated 2026-04-08)

### A.1 Sources

This summary draws on In-vitro Drug Interaction Report IV-DDI-VLT-003 v1.0, on
studies VLT-1005 and VLT-1006, and on PBPK-VLT-001.

### A.2 Veltrapib as a victim

Veltrapib is eliminated predominantly by CYP3A4.

Cyprazole is a strong CYP3A inhibitor.
Co-administration of cyprazole reduced veltrapib exposure.
A dose-reduction instruction is proposed for concomitant use with strong CYP3A
inhibitors.

### A.3 Veltrapib as a perpetrator

Time-dependent inhibition of CYP2B6 was not further evaluated.

Veltrapib is a weak inhibitor of CYP3A. In study VLT-1006 the geometric mean
ratio for vorastol AUC0–inf with veltrapib was 2.14.

For OATP1B1 substrates, the clinical study showed no effect of clinical
relevance, with a ratio of 1.09.

No interaction is expected with P-gp substrates.

In one additional comparison, co-administration increased exposure by 1.8-fold.

Veltrapib did not induce CYP1A2, CYP2B6 or CYP3A4 in human hepatocytes.
