## VLT-1005 — Effect of cyprazole, a strong CYP3A inhibitor, on veltrapib (CSR v1.0)

**Design.** Open-label, fixed sequence. Veltrapib 20 mg single dose alone, then
veltrapib 20 mg with cyprazole 200 mg once daily at steady state.
**Perpetrator:** cyprazole. **Victim:** veltrapib.

### Table 3.1 — Veltrapib exposure with and without cyprazole

| Parameter | Geometric mean ratio (with cyprazole / alone) | 90% CI |
|---|---|---|
| AUC0–inf | 4.62 | 3.98–5.36 |
| Cmax | 2.85 | 2.44–3.33 |

Veltrapib exposure was higher when administered with cyprazole.

## PBPK-VLT-001 — Physiologically based pharmacokinetic simulation report (v1.0)

**Purpose.** To simulate the effect of veltrapib on the pharmacokinetics of an
OATP1B1 substrate, in place of a clinical interaction study.

**Status.** This report describes a PBPK simulation. **No clinical interaction
study of veltrapib with an OATP1B1 substrate has been conducted.**

**Simulated result.** The simulated AUC ratio for the OATP1B1 substrate
co-administered with veltrapib 40 mg once daily was 1.09 (5th–95th percentile
1.02–1.19).

**Model inputs.** OATP1B1 IC50 2.1 µM from IV-DDI-VLT-003 v2.0; unbound hepatic
inlet concentration derived as described in section 4 of that report.
