## Part B — Commercial presentation strengths

Commercial presentation: 50 mg and 200 mg tablets only.

The tablets are film-coated and must not be split.

Doses obtainable from the commercial presentation: 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300, 350, 400 mg and further multiples of 50 mg.
