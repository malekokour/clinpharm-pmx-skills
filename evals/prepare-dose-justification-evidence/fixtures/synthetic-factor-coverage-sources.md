## Part A — Renal category definitions used in this programme

Category definitions used in this programme: normal ≥90; mild 60–89; moderate 30–59; severe 15–29; kidney failure <15 (mL/min/1.73 m²).

These definitions are the ones the programme applies. No other staging scheme is
used anywhere in this package.
