## Dossier content and style conventions (I10)

- **Exposure values are reported to three significant figures.** This applies to
  AUC, Cmax and any derived exposure metric in Modules 2.7.2, 2.7.3 and 2.7.4.
- **Concentrations are reported in ng/mL** and exposures in ng·h/mL. No
  concentration is reported in µg/mL anywhere in Module 2.
- **Dose nomenclature** is written as amount, unit, then frequency in words —
  for example "60 mg once daily". A regimen is never abbreviated to "QD" or "BID"
  in a Module 2 summary.
- **Rounding tolerance for cross-document reconciliation: 0.5%.**
- Every cross-reference resolves to a numbered appendix, a Module 5 study number,
  or a named report that exists in the version baseline below.
