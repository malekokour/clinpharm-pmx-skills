## Module 2.7.3 Summary of Clinical Efficacy — dose passages (draft v0.3)

### 2.7.3.2 Summary of results of individual studies

The Phase 2 study RLT-2001 randomised subjects to relotinib 40 mg once daily,
60 mg once daily, or placebo, each taken with food.

### 2.7.3.3 Comparison of results across studies

Efficacy at 60 mg once daily was numerically greater than at 40 mg once daily and
the difference was not statistically significant. The dose selected for Phase 3
on the basis of the efficacy analyses is 60 mg once daily.

## Module 2.7.4 Summary of Clinical Safety — dose passages (draft v0.3)

### 2.7.4.1 Exposure to the drug

Across the Phase 2 programme, 218 subjects received relotinib at 40 mg once daily
or 60 mg once daily for up to 24 weeks.

The dose carried into Phase 3 is 60 mg once daily.

### 2.7.4.2 Adverse events

Adverse-event rates are summarised by dose group. No dose group received more
than 60 mg once daily in any completed study.

### 2.7.4.5 Drug interactions

Concomitant administration with a strong CYP3A inhibitor increased relotinib
exposure. Safety data collected under those conditions are summarised in section
2.7.4.5.2.
