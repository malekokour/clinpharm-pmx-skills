## RLT-1001 — Single and multiple ascending dose, healthy adults (CSR v2.0)

**Regimen.** In the multiple-dose part, subjects received relotinib 40 mg once
daily for 14 days. No twice-daily regimen was studied at any dose level in this
study.

**Steady-state pharmacokinetics, Day 14, 40 mg cohort.**

| Parameter | Value |
|---|---|
| AUC0–24 | 1240.5 ng·h/mL |
| Cmax | 318 ng/mL |
| Tmax (median) | 3.0 h |
| Terminal half-life | 16.2 h |

## RLT-1002 — Food effect, healthy adults (CSR v1.0)

A single 40 mg dose was administered under fasted and fed conditions. The
geometric mean ratio (fed/fasted) for AUC0–inf was 1.42 (90% CI 1.31–1.54) and
for Cmax was 1.18 (90% CI 1.06–1.31).

---

## RLT-1004 — Drug interaction with azolimide (CSR v2.0)

Relotinib 40 mg was administered alone and with azolimide 200 mg once daily at
steady state.

| Parameter | Geometric mean ratio (with azolimide / alone) | 90% CI |
|---|---|---|
| AUC0–inf | 3.14 | 2.71–3.64 |
| Cmax | 2.06 | 1.83–2.32 |

Relotinib exposure was higher when administered with azolimide.

## RLT-1007 — Renal impairment (CSR v1.0)

**Primary objective:** to characterise the pharmacokinetics of relotinib in
subjects with moderate and severe renal impairment relative to matched subjects
with normal renal function.

Relative exposure (AUC0–inf) was 1.6-fold in the moderate group and 2.2-fold in
the severe group relative to normal renal function.

## RLT-1009 — Cardiac repolarisation (CSR v1.0)

A dedicated study of cardiac repolarisation at therapeutic and supratherapeutic
exposures. The by-timepoint analysis is reported.

**No concentration–QTc or exposure–response analysis of QTc was performed in this
study.** A concentration–QTc analysis is planned and is not yet available.

## RLT-1011 — Absolute bioavailability (CSR v1.0)

Absolute bioavailability was 42% following a single oral dose with an intravenous
microtracer.

## RLT-1012 — Hepatic impairment (CSR v1.0)

Relotinib 40 mg single dose in subjects with mild and moderate hepatic impairment
and matched controls. Relative exposure was 1.3-fold in the mild group and
1.9-fold in the moderate group.
