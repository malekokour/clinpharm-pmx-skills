## Section 4.1 — Clinical pharmacology programme overview

The clinical pharmacology programme comprises seven studies. In the
dose-ranging programme, three dose levels were evaluated before the proposed
registration dose was selected.

## Section 4.2 — Exposure–response, efficacy

**Position P1.** Efficacy increases with steady-state exposure across the
studied range. Geometric mean AUC0–24,ss at the proposed dose was
2480 ng·h/mL. Supporting evidence: Annex A-01, Table 3.

**Position P2.** Exposure at the proposed dose in the pivotal study population
was consistent with the dose-ranging programme. Geometric mean AUC0–24,ss in
the pivotal population was 3120 ng·h/mL. Supporting evidence: Annex A-03.

The exposure–response slope was 0.84 with a 90% confidence interval of
0.71 to 1.06.

## Section 4.3 — Exposure–response, safety

**Position P3.** Hepatic safety does not require a dedicated study. At the
End-of-Phase-2 meeting the Agency agreed that no dedicated hepatic impairment study is required.
Supporting evidence: Annex A-04.

## Section 4.4 — Intrinsic factors

**Position P4.** Renal function does not materially alter exposure. Supporting
evidence: Annex A-06, physiologically based pharmacokinetic report, Section 5.

**Position P5.** Body weight was evaluated as a covariate and did not warrant
dosing by weight band. Supporting evidence: Annex A-12, covariate forest plot.

**Position P6.** No dedicated paediatric pharmacokinetic data are available. A
partial waiver request is planned and the applicant acknowledges the gap.
Supporting evidence: Annex A-02, Section 2.

## Section 4.5 — Extrinsic factors

**Position P7.** Co-administration with a strong CYP3A4 inhibitor increases
exposure. Derived exposure metrics are taken from Population PK Report v2.0.
Supporting evidence: Annex 3, Table 1.

## Section 4.6 — Formulation bridging

**Position P8.** The commercial tablet is bridged to the clinical capsule.
Supporting evidence: Annex A-05.
