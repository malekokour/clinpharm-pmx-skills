# SYNTHETIC — Study PHX-101, PK Analysis Plan extract  (v1.0)

- AUC calculated by the linear-up/log-down trapezoidal method.
- Terminal half-life estimated over at least three sampling points in the
  terminal phase, with adjusted R² ≥ 0.80.
- **Concentration and exposure values reported to three significant figures.**
- Clearance reported in **L/h**.
- Dose proportionality assessed by the power model over the **full studied dose
  range**. Any claim restricted to a sub-range requires its own analysis.
- Rounding tolerance for cross-document reconciliation: **0.5%**.
