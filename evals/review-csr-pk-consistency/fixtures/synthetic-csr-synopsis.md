## §2.1 Study design

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled multiple ascending dose study.
Four cohorts: 50, 100, 200, 400 mg once daily for 14 days. n=8 per cohort
(6 active, 2 placebo). All 32 enrolled subjects were included in the PK
population.

## §2.3 Pharmacokinetic results

Following multiple dosing, steady state was achieved by Day 7. Mean AUC0–τ at
steady state in the 200 mg cohort was **11300 ng·h/mL**. Mean Cmax was
1206 ng/mL with a median Tmax of **2.0 h**.

Terminal half-life was approximately **8.2 h** and the observed accumulation
ratio was **1.9**.

Exposure was **dose-proportional over the 50–200 mg range**.

Administration with a high-fat meal produced a **decrease** in Cmax
(geometric mean ratio 1.34, 90% CI 1.18–1.52).
