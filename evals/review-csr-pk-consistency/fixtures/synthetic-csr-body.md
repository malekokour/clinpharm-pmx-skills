# SYNTHETIC — Study PHX-101 CSR, PK body sections  (v3.0)

## §9.3 Methods — dosing

Subjects received synitinib at **50, 100, 200 and 400 mg** once daily for
14 days. Dose levels were as specified in the original protocol.

## §9.5.4 Bioanalytical methods

Plasma concentrations were determined using a validated LC-MS/MS method described
in **Bioanalytical Validation Report BA-2024-017 v1.0**.

## §11.2 Analysis populations

The PK population comprised all subjects receiving at least one dose with
evaluable concentration data. **Cohort 4 subjects 401 and 407 were excluded from
the steady-state summary** following protocol-defined dosing non-compliance.

## §12.2 Absorption

Median Tmax was **3.5 h** across cohorts (range 1.0–6.0 h).

## §12.3 Distribution and elimination

Mean terminal half-life was **11.4 h**. Apparent clearance was 15.2 L/h.
The accumulation ratio at steady state was 1.9.
