## Notes on identifiers

Study numbers in this programme run ZLV-101 through ZLV-108 with ZLV-105 and
ZLV-106 unassigned. **No study numbered ZLV-105 exists in this programme.**
ZLV-106 was reserved for a mass-balance study that was never initiated.

The correct identifier for the interaction study is ZLV-104. Transposed forms
appearing elsewhere refer to the same study.
