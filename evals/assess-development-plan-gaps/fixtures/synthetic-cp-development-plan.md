## §3 Programme summary

The programme comprises eight clinical pharmacology studies spanning
first-in-human dose escalation, food effect, drug–drug interaction and
dose expansion. The full listing is held in the study inventory, which is the
denominator for coverage.

## §4 Planned and completed clinical pharmacology work

### §4.1 First-in-human and dose escalation

Study ZLV-101 (single and multiple ascending dose) is complete. Study ZLV-102
(dose expansion, steady-state pharmacokinetics) has completed enrolment;
ZLV-102 results are pending and will be incorporated at the next revision.

### §4.2 Food effect

Study ZLV-103 evaluated a high-fat meal against the fasted state. ZLV-103 is
complete and reported; no further food-effect work is planned.

### §4.3 Drug–drug interaction

One clinical interaction study, ZLV-104, covers the interaction package.
ZLV-104 administers zelvatinib with itraconazole to characterise zelvatinib as
an interaction victim. Enrolment for ZVL-104 completed in May 2026.

No further clinical interaction work is planned. The in-vitro package is
considered to close the remaining interaction questions.

### §4.4 Elimination and physicochemical properties

Zelvatinib is cleared predominantly by hepatic metabolism. Renal elimination is
minor (fe < 10%), and no dedicated renal impairment study is planned.

Aqueous solubility at pH 6.8 is 0.8 mg/mL. Plasma protein binding is high
(>99%), consistent with the in-vitro dossier.

### §4.5 Hepatic impairment

Hepatic impairment will be addressed by population pharmacokinetic covariate
analysis using pooled data from ZLV-101 and ZLV-102.
No dedicated hepatic study is planned.

### §4.6 Cardiac repolarisation

The QT assessment expectation is met by the ZLV-105 thorough QT study, reported
in 2025.

### §4.7 Mass balance

A human mass-balance study has not been performed. The sponsor's position on
this element is recorded in the waiver rationale file.
