# SYN-101 Internal Scientific Briefing Outline

## 1. Purpose

- Summarize the completed synthetic population PK analysis of PX-101.
- Identify the next model-informed development question for cross-functional
  discussion.
- This educational example is not a dosing recommendation.

## 2. Key results

- Final apparent clearance: 14.2 L/h. The 12.4 L/h value in the project brief
  was a planning assumption, not the completed estimate.
- Final apparent volume: 128 L.
- Body weight was retained on clearance using fixed allometric scaling.
- The exploratory renal-function effect was imprecise.
- Formulation was not retained in the final synthetic model.
- Final parameter uncertainty is summarized with 90% confidence intervals, not
  the 80% interval stated in the unapproved draft.
- Participants with severe renal impairment were not represented.

## 3. Interpretation

- The analysis supports describing PK in the represented synthetic population.
- The available evidence does not support the draft claim that no dose
  adjustment is needed in severe renal impairment.
- The draft's statement that body weight was the only clinically important
  covariate is stronger than the analysis summary, which reports model
  retention and an imprecise exploratory renal-function effect.
- Dose or labeling language requires qualified scientific, medical, regulatory,
  and statistical review.

## 4. Recommendation for the next analysis

- The team should explicitly decide between the two open options: a broad
  renal-function sensitivity scenario or a simulation limited to the observed
  population.
- Until that decision is made, do not present either option as the agreed next
  analysis. If the broad sensitivity scenario is selected, frame it as
  exploratory because severe renal impairment was absent from the dataset.

## 5. Conclusion

The completed synthetic analysis estimates apparent clearance at 14.2 L/h and
apparent volume at 128 L, retains fixed allometric body-weight scaling on
clearance, and does not retain formulation. Renal-function findings remain
imprecise, and the evidence does not support conclusions about dosing in severe
renal impairment. The next simulation remains an open team decision, and the
briefing requires the stated scientific reviews before external use.
