# SYN-101 Internal Scientific Briefing Outline

## 1. Purpose

- Summarize the completed synthetic population PK analysis for PX-101.
- Describe apparent clearance and volume, the evaluated covariates, and the remaining model-informed development question.
- This educational fixture is not a dosing recommendation and requires scientific review before external use.

## 2. Key results

- Final apparent clearance estimate: **14.2 L/h**. The **12.4 L/h** value in the project brief was a planning assumption, not the completed estimate.
- Final apparent volume estimate: **128 L**.
- Body weight was retained on clearance using fixed allometric scaling.
- The exploratory renal-function effect was imprecise.
- Formulation was not retained in the final synthetic model.
- Parameter uncertainty in the final table uses **90% confidence intervals**.
- Participants with severe renal impairment were not represented.

## 3. Interpretation

- The completed analysis supports describing the represented synthetic population.
- The analysis does not support a conclusion about dosing in severe renal impairment because that population was not represented.
- The unapproved draft conflicts with the governing analysis summary when it cites an 80% confidence interval and concludes that no renal-impairment dose adjustment is needed.
- The statement that body weight was the only clinically important covariate is stronger than the source evidence, which states only that body weight was retained and the renal-function effect was imprecise.

## 4. Recommendation for the next analysis

- Resolve the still-open analysis question before proceeding: whether to evaluate a broad renal-function sensitivity scenario or focus only on the observed population.
- If a broad sensitivity analysis is selected, state its extrapolative assumptions and uncertainty explicitly; do not treat it as observed evidence for severe renal impairment.
- Obtain review from the fictional pharmacometrics lead and clinical pharmacology lead. Any dose or labeling language also requires qualified scientific, medical, regulatory, and statistical review.

## 5. Conclusion

The completed synthetic analysis estimates apparent clearance at 14.2 L/h and apparent volume at 128 L, with body weight retained on clearance. It does not establish a dosing conclusion for severe renal impairment. The next simulation scope remains undecided and should be confirmed through qualified scientific review.
