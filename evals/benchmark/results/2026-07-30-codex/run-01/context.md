## 3. Interpretation limited to represented evidence

- The completed analysis supports describing the represented synthetic population. `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- The available evidence does not support a dosing conclusion for severe renal impairment because that population was not represented. `[Analysis-Summary.md; Project-Brief.md]`
- The imprecise exploratory renal-function effect should be reported with its limitation; it should not be translated into evidence of no effect. `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- Body weight was retained in the final model, but the sources do not establish that it was the only clinically important covariate. `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- This educational artifact is not clinical evidence or a dosing recommendation. `[Project-Brief.md; AI Working Pack]`
