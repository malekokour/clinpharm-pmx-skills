## §6.3 PD analysis

BMR-7 is analysed in plasma at pre-dose and 2 h, 8 h, and 24 h post-dose using
assay PD-EL-007 v2.0. A PD responder is defined as an increase of at least
**25%** from baseline.

The SAP does not state that BMR-7 is qualified, clinically meaningful, or a
surrogate and does not connect the category to a dose change.
