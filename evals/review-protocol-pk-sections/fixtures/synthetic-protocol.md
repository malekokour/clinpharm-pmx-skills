## Synopsis extract

Randomised, double-blind, placebo-controlled **multiple ascending dose** study of
calvirostat in healthy adult participants. Four cohorts, 14 days of once-daily
dosing, n=10 per cohort (8 active, 2 placebo).

**PK profiles will be collected on Days 1 and 14.** Trough samples are collected
on intervening days as specified in the Schedule of Assessments.

## §3 Objectives and endpoints

### §3.1 Pharmacokinetic objectives

To characterise the single- and multiple-dose pharmacokinetics of calvirostat.

Primary PK endpoints: **AUC0-tau**, Cmax, Tmax, **terminal half-life (t1/2z)**,
apparent clearance (CL/F) and accumulation ratio.

## §5.4 Participant restrictions

Participants must abstain from:

- alcohol from 48 h before the first dose until the final PK sample;
- caffeine-containing products from 12 h before each PK profile day;
- grapefruit and grapefruit juice from 7 days before the first dose;
- strenuous exercise from 24 h before each PK profile day.

*(No further restriction is specified in this section.)*

## §6.1 Dose levels

| Cohort | Dose level | Regimen |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 50 mg | once daily, 14 days |
| 2 | 150 mg | once daily, 14 days |
| 3 | 300 mg | once daily, 14 days |
| 4 | **600 mg** | once daily, 14 days |

Escalation to the next cohort follows review of safety and available PK data by
the dose escalation committee.

**Stopping rules.** Escalation stops, and no higher cohort is dosed, if any of
the following occurs: a dose-limiting toxicity in two or more participants in a
cohort; a mean steady-state Cmax exceeding the exposure cap defined in the
Investigator's Brochure; or a committee decision to hold on any other ground.

## §6.2 Administration

Study drug is administered with 240 mL of water. On Day 1 the dose is
administered after an overnight fast of at least 8 h. On Days 2 to 14 the dose is
administered at approximately the same clock time each day.

### §9.4.2 Basis for the sampling duration

The 24 h post-final-dose sampling duration was selected on the basis of a
**terminal half-life of approximately 6.2 h** reported in the Investigator's
Brochure, which is expected to provide adequate characterisation of the terminal
phase.

### §9.4.3 Sample handling and shipping

Blood samples are collected into K2EDTA tubes, inverted, and centrifuged at
1500 g for 10 min at 4 °C within 30 min of collection. Plasma is transferred to
two aliquots, frozen upright at -70 °C within 60 min, and shipped on dry ice to
the bioanalytical laboratory with a temperature logger in each shipment.

## §9.5 Bioanalysis

Plasma calvirostat concentrations will be determined using validated LC-MS/MS
method **BA-M-0142**, with a lower limit of quantification of **0.25 µg/mL**.

Assay performance and validation status are described in the bioanalytical method
summary and in **Investigator's Brochure Edition 3.0**.

## §9.6 Pharmacokinetic analysis plan (embedded)

Non-compartmental analysis will be performed on plasma concentration-time data.

Parameters to be derived: **AUC0-24**, Cmax, Tmax, t1/2z, CL/F, Vz/F and
accumulation ratio (RAUC).

AUC will be calculated by the linear-up/log-down trapezoidal method. Terminal
half-life will be estimated by log-linear regression over at least three points
in the terminal phase.

Concentrations will be reported in ng/mL, exposure in ng·h/mL and apparent
clearance in L/h. Concentration and exposure values will be reported to **two
significant figures**.

Missing samples will be reported as missing and not imputed. Protocol deviations
affecting a PK sample will be listed with the nominal and actual time. A profile
may be excluded from a summary only for a documented dosing deviation or emesis
within two times the median Tmax, in each case recorded with its reason.

The population PK modelling strategy is **deliberately deferred to a standalone
modelling analysis plan**, to be finalised before database lock. This deferral is
intentional and is recorded here so that it is not read as an omission.
