## 5.2 Human pharmacokinetics — study CVS-100 (single ascending dose)

| Parameter | Value observed across 25-400 mg |
|---|---|
| Median Tmax | 3.0 h |
| **Mean terminal half-life** | **18.6 h** |
| AUC0-inf range | 640-11400 ng·h/mL |
| Cmax range | 41-780 ng/mL |
| Apparent clearance (CL/F) | 33-42 L/h |

Terminal half-life was estimated over at least four points in the terminal phase
in every profile contributing to the estimate.

Edition 3.0 of this brochure reported a preliminary half-life estimate derived
from the two lowest dose cohorts only. **Edition 4.0 supersedes it**; the
Edition 4.0 estimate is the one to be used for design purposes.

## 5.4 Basis for the clinical dose levels

Dose levels for study CVS-101 were derived from the 13-week repeat-dose toxicology
study in the rat and the 4-week study in the dog, with exposure margins as follows:

| Clinical dose level | Derivation source | Predicted exposure margin to NOAEL |
|---|---|---|
| 50 mg | Rat 13-week NOAEL, MRSD approach | 42-fold |
| 150 mg | Rat 13-week NOAEL, allometric projection | 14-fold |
| 300 mg | Dog 4-week NOAEL, allometric projection | 6.1-fold |

*No derivation is recorded in this brochure for any dose level above 300 mg.*

## 5.6 Absorption and food

A dedicated food-effect study has not been performed. In study CVS-100, exposure
after a high-fat breakfast was numerically higher than under fasted conditions in
an exploratory sub-cohort; the sponsor recommends that **meal conditions be
standardised in any study deriving Cmax**.
