## Section 2 — Dosage and administration

The recommended dosage is 100 mg once daily, taken orally.

## Section 12.3 — Pharmacokinetics

### Distribution and elimination

Apparent clearance is 16.8 L/h. Apparent volume of distribution is 268 L. The
terminal half-life is 14 h.

### Steady-state exposure

At the recommended dosage, mean steady-state peak concentration is 1240 µg/mL
and mean area under the concentration–time curve over the dosing interval is
4210 ng·h/mL.

### Effect of food

Administration with a high-fat meal increases exposure.

### Specific populations

No dosage adjustment is proposed on the basis of age, sex or body weight.
