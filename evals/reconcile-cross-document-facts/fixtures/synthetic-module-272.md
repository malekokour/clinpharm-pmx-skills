> Fully synthetic summarising document. Draft under review.

## §2.7.2.1 Background and overview

Dorvastine is an orally administered small molecule. The clinical pharmacology
programme comprises three studies: DVS-101, DVS-102 and DVS-103.

Absolute bioavailability is 62%.

## §2.7.2.2 Summary of results of individual studies

Steady-state pharmacokinetic parameters are summarised from the DVS-101 CSR v1.0.

| Parameter | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Apparent clearance (CL/F) | 18.4 L/h | DVS-101 §12.3 |
| AUC0–24,ss, 100 mg once daily | 4210.00 ng·h/mL | DVS-101 §12.4 |
| Cmax,ss, 100 mg once daily | 1240 ng/mL | DVS-101 §12.4 |
| Terminal half-life | 14 h | DVS-101 §12.3 |

The population pharmacokinetic analysis dataset comprised 412 subjects.

## §2.7.2.3 Comparison and analyses of results across studies

Steady state is achieved by Day 8, based on trough concentration profiles.

Administration with a high-fat meal produced a 34% decrease in AUC relative to
the fasted state. Source: Study DVS-102.

## §2.7.2.4 Special studies

No special studies were conducted.
