## §7.4 Pharmacokinetic sample collection

Blood samples for quexatinib and metabolite M1 are collected into K2EDTA tubes at
pre-dose and at 0.5, 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 12 and 24 hours after dosing on Days 1 and
14, and pre-dose on Days 4, 7 and 10.

## §7.5 Sample handling

Samples are placed on wet ice within 5 minutes of collection, centrifuged within
30 minutes at 4 °C, and the plasma aliquoted into two polypropylene tubes.

Total time from collection to freezing must not exceed 2 hours.

## §7.6 Storage and shipment

Aliquots are stored at the site at −70 °C and shipped to the bioanalytical
laboratory on dry ice.

Samples are retained at −70 °C at the bioanalytical laboratory until analysis.
The protocol places no upper limit on the interval between collection and
analysis; the interval actually achieved is recorded in the study sample analysis
report.

## §7.7 Metabolite scope

Metabolite M1 is quantified for exploratory purposes only. Parent quexatinib is
the analyte of record for all pharmacokinetic parameters reported in the study
report.
