# SYNTHETIC — Study Sample Analysis Report BA-SSA-2025-088 (v1.0), Study ZLV-102

**Study:** ZLV-102, multiple ascending dose, quexatinib
**Analyte:** quexatinib and metabolite M1 in human K2EDTA plasma
**Report version:** v1.0, issued 2026-02-20

## §1 Purpose

This report describes the analysis of pharmacokinetic samples collected in study
ZLV-102 and the performance of the assay during that analysis.

## §2 Method reference

Analysis was performed using the LC-MS/MS method described in Bioanalytical
Method Validation Report BA-VAL-2025-041 v1.0.

## §3 Sample receipt, handling and storage

### §3.1 Receipt

Samples were received in 11 shipments between 2024-09-03 and 2025-01-18.

### §3.2 Shipping condition

Samples from Cohort 2 were received at +4 °C rather than on dry ice, a documented
shipping deviation recorded on receipt form RF-ZLV-0044. The samples were
refrozen on receipt and analysed.

### §3.3 Storage before analysis

Study samples were stored at −70 °C for up to 14 months before analysis. The
earliest sample collected was analysed 14 months after collection; the median
storage interval across the study was 7 months.

## §4 Assay performance during study sample analysis

The LLOQ applied to study samples was 0.250 ng/mL for quexatinib.

A total of 84 analytical runs were performed. Seventy-nine runs were accepted at
first pass. Five runs were rejected on quality-control failure and reassayed; all
five reassays were accepted.

## §5 Reporting of values below the limit of quantification

Concentrations below the limit of quantification were reported as 0.00 ng/mL in
the concentration dataset supplied to pharmacokinetics.

## §6 Samples above the upper limit of quantification

46 samples exceeded the upper limit of quantification and were reanalysed after
20-fold dilution.

A further 4 samples exceeded the upper limit of quantification by a smaller
margin and were reanalysed after 10-fold dilution.

## §7 Repeats and reassays

12 samples required repeat analysis for chromatographic reasons. Because these
samples had already been thawed for the original run and for two reassays, they
underwent up to 5 freeze-thaw cycles before the reported value was obtained.

The reported value for each repeated sample is the value from the accepted repeat
run, per the laboratory's reported-value selection rule stated in §11.

## §8 Incurred sample reanalysis

Seventy sample pairs were selected for incurred sample reanalysis, drawn from the
peak and terminal phases across all four cohorts.

## §9 Deviations

No protocol deviations affected sample analysis.

## §10 Carryover during study sample analysis

Not assessed during study sample analysis. Refer to the validation report.

## §11 Reported-value selection rule

Where a sample was analysed more than once, the reported value is the value from
the run that met all acceptance criteria. Where more than one run met all
criteria, the first accepted result is reported.
