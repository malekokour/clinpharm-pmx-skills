# SYNTHETIC — Study ZLV-102 PK Analysis Plan extract (PK-AP-ZLV-102 v2.0)

> Fully synthetic. Generated for evaluation only.

## §3.1 Concentration conventions

- Concentrations are reported in **ng/mL** throughout. No plasma concentration is
  reported in µg/mL in any table, listing or figure.
- Concentrations are reported to three significant figures.
- The analyte of record is parent quexatinib. Metabolite M1 concentrations are
  summarised separately and are not used in any parameter derivation.

## §3.2 Handling of values below the limit of quantification

Concentrations below the limit of quantification are **set to missing** in the
pharmacokinetic analysis dataset. They are not imputed as zero, and they are not
imputed as half the limit of quantification, at any position in the profile.

Where the bioanalytical dataset supplies a numeric value for a below-limit
result, that value is replaced with a missing code before parameter derivation,
and the substitution is recorded in the analysis log.

## §3.3 Limit of quantification

The limit of quantification used for pharmacokinetic analysis is the one stated
in the authoritative version of the bioanalytical method validation report. Where
the study sample analysis report states a different limit, the discrepancy is
resolved by the clinical pharmacology lead before parameter derivation, not by
the analyst.

## §3.4 Rounding tolerance for cross-document reconciliation

Rounding tolerance for cross-document reconciliation: **0.5%**.

## §3.5 Parameters derived

AUC0–τ, Cmax, Tmax, terminal half-life, apparent clearance and accumulation
ratio, by non-compartmental analysis.
