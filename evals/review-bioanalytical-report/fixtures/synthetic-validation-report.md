**Analyte:** quexatinib (parent) and its metabolite M1
**Matrix:** human K2EDTA plasma
**Method:** LC-MS/MS following protein precipitation
**Report version:** v2.0, issued 2025-11-14 (supersedes v1.0 of 2025-06-02)
**Governing standard declared by the sponsor:** ICH M10

## 1. Method description and analyte definition

Quexatinib and metabolite M1 are quantified simultaneously in human K2EDTA plasma
using a stable-isotope-labelled internal standard for each analyte. Sample volume
is 50 µL. Chromatographic run time is 4.2 minutes.

Quexatinib is the analyte of record for the pharmacokinetic dataset. M1 is
quantified as a secondary analyte with its own calibration curve and its own
quality-control set.

## 2. Calibration curve range and model

The calibration range for quexatinib is 0.500 to 500 ng/mL, fitted with a
weighted (1/x²) linear regression. The lower limit of quantification (LLOQ) for
quexatinib is 0.500 ng/mL. The LLOQ for metabolite M1 is 1.00 ng/mL over a
calibration range of 1.00 to 250 ng/mL.

## 3. Accuracy and precision, within and between run

Quality-control concentrations: LLOQ QC 0.500 ng/mL, low QC 1.50 ng/mL, mid QC
40.0 ng/mL, high QC 400 ng/mL. The QC concentrations are set independently of the
calibration standard concentrations, as the laboratory's standard practice.

## 4. Selectivity and specificity

Six independent lots of blank human K2EDTA plasma were screened. No interfering
peak exceeding 20% of the LLOQ response was observed at the retention time of
either analyte or either internal standard. Haemolysed and lipaemic lots were
each screened separately and are reported in Appendix 2.

## 5. Matrix effect and recovery

Matrix factor was determined in six lots at low and high QC. The internal-standard
normalised matrix factor CV was 4.8% for quexatinib and 5.6% for M1. Mean
extraction recovery was 88.4% for quexatinib and 84.1% for M1, consistent across
the concentration range.

## 6. Dilution integrity

Dilution integrity was demonstrated at 10-fold dilution using a nominal
concentration of 5,000 ng/mL diluted into blank matrix (n = 6). Mean bias was
−3.2% with a CV of 4.1%. No higher dilution factor was assessed in this
validation.

## 7. Stability

| Stability type | Conditions demonstrated | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Stock solution | 30 days at −20 °C | Mean difference −1.8% |
| Short-term (bench top) | 6 hours at room temperature | Mean bias −4.2% |
| Long-term (matrix) | 9 months at −70 °C | Mean bias −6.1% |
| Freeze-thaw | 3 freeze-thaw cycles, −70 °C to room temperature | Mean bias −5.4% |
| Processed sample (autosampler) | 48 hours at 6 °C | Mean bias +2.2% |

Long-term matrix stability is demonstrated to 9 months at −70 °C. No longer
storage interval was assessed in this validation.

Short-term bench-top stability of 6 hours describes handling at room temperature
before freezing; it is a separate condition from the long-term storage interval
above and does not bound it.

## 10. Deviations and their impact assessment

Two deviations occurred during validation: an autosampler temperature excursion
during run 4 (reported and repeated) and a delayed extraction in run 7. Both are
described in Appendix 6 with an impact assessment concluding that neither
affected the reported validation parameters.

## 11. Acceptance criteria applied by this laboratory

- Calibration standards: at least 75% of standards within ±15% of nominal (±20% at the LLOQ).
- Quality controls: mean bias within ±15% of nominal (±20% at the LLOQ QC).
- Incurred sample reanalysis: at least two-thirds of repeat results within ±20% of the original result.
