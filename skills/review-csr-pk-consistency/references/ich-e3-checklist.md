## Boundaries

Structural conformance only. This checklist does not assess whether the PK
analysis was appropriate, whether conclusions are justified, or whether the study
met its objectives. A present-but-wrong section passes structural conformance and
fails the content checks elsewhere in the workflow — the two are separate
questions and must not be conflated in reporting.
