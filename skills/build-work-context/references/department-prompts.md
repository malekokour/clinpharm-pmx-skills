# Department-specific interview prompts

Read only the selected function. These are optional probes, not facts about the
user or universal descriptions of each department.

## Clinical pharmacology and pharmacometrics

- Which decisions do you support: development strategy, dose selection,
  labeling, study design, analysis review, or regulatory response?
- Which analysis artifacts govern your work?
- What constitutes reproducible evidence in your environment?
- Which conclusions require pharmacometrician, clinician, statistician, or
  regulatory sign-off?
- How should model assumptions, uncertainty, and source traceability appear in
  deliverables?

## Biostatistics and data science

- Which study phases, endpoints, and analysis types are in scope?
- What are the governing protocol, SAP, data standards, and programming
  conventions?
- Which outputs require independent programming or statistical review?
- How should estimands, populations, missing data, and multiplicity be handled
  in summaries?
- Which tools and validated environments are permitted?

## Clinical development and operations

- Which studies, countries, vendors, and milestones are in scope?
- What is the authority order among protocol, plans, systems, trackers, and
  meeting decisions?
- Which operational changes require study-team, quality, medical, or regulatory
  approval?
- Which risks, dependencies, and actions should be surfaced first?
- Which systems remain operational truth rather than the generated context?

## Regulatory affairs

- Which jurisdictions, submission types, and product stages are in scope?
- Which approved documents and agency interactions govern the response?
- How should commitments, questions, comments, and response ownership be
  tracked?
- Which statements require legal, medical, statistical, or functional review?
- What must remain draft or prepare-only?

## Medical affairs

- Which audience and scientific-exchange boundary applies?
- Which approved evidence and reference set governs?
- What distinguishes scientific response, medical education, field insight, and
  promotional use?
- Which outputs require medical, legal, regulatory, or compliance review?
- How should balanced evidence, limitations, and citations appear?

## Pharmacovigilance and safety

- Which case, signal, aggregate-report, or governance workflow is in scope?
- Which controlled terminology and safety systems govern?
- What are the escalation, seriousness, expectedness, and timing boundaries?
- Which judgments require a safety physician or qualified reviewer?
- What information is prohibited from the current environment?

## CMC, quality, and manufacturing

- Which product, process, site, lifecycle stage, and quality system are in scope?
- Which specifications, methods, records, procedures, and change controls govern?
- Which activities occur in a validated environment?
- Which deviations, risks, or decisions require quality-unit approval?
- How should traceability, version, and effective-date information appear?

## Market access, commercial, and strategy

- Which market, audience, product stage, and decision are in scope?
- Which claims, assumptions, forecasts, and evidence sources govern?
- What separates strategic exploration from approved external messaging?
- Which outputs require medical, legal, regulatory, privacy, or brand review?
- What level of uncertainty and scenario framing is required?
