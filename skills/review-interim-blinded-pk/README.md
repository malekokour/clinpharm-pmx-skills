# Interim and Blinded PK Review

Owns the job-model row **Interim and blinded PK review**. Split from `review-study-conduct-pk` under PS-D030.

Looking has a cost that is usually discovered later: a person who has seen unblinded exposure data cannot afterwards make a blinded judgment about a deviation, an exclusion or an amendment. This names who becomes conflicted, before the look.
