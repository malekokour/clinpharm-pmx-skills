Every mismatch preserves both statements and both locators. `UNKNOWN` names the
missing source, version, applicability input, or owner declaration that would
resolve it. A blank cell is not a pass.

## Human-only boundary

This trace does not judge consent adequacy, validity, voluntariness, participant
understanding, coercion, acceptable burden, future-use acceptability, or whether
re-consent is required. It does not classify vulnerability or capacity. Every
row remains open for the named human reviewers.
