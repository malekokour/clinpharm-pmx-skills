# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

ClinPharm PMx Skills welcomes respectful, evidence-led participation across
scientific, medical, regulatory, operational, technical, and commercial
disciplines.

Contributors must:

- discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct errors openly;
- respect professional, cultural, and disciplinary differences;
- avoid presenting generated material as qualified medical or regulatory
  judgment; and
- protect confidential, patient-level, proprietary, and personal information.

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, deliberate misinformation,
and repeated disregard of repository safety boundaries are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this
standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Do not put
sensitive evidence in a public report. Use GitHub's private vulnerability
reporting route for security or data-exposure concerns.

This policy is adapted from the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1:
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
