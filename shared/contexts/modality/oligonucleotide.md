# Context — oligonucleotide

**Dimension:** modality · **Attaches after selection; never selected**

Covers antisense oligonucleotides, siRNA, and conjugated forms. The recurring error is
treating plasma exposure as the exposure that matters.

## Plasma is not where the drug works

Plasma concentrations fall rapidly while tissue concentrations persist for weeks to
months. The consequences:

- **Plasma half-life and effect duration are decoupled.** A short plasma half-life is
  compatible with quarterly or twice-yearly dosing.
- **Plasma AUC is a poor exposure metric for effect.** Where an exposure–response
  relationship is built on plasma alone, say so as a limitation rather than reporting it
  as the relationship.
- **Steady state in plasma does not imply steady state at the target tissue.**
- Accumulation is a **tissue** phenomenon; a plasma accumulation ratio near unity does
  not mean no accumulation.

Pharmacodynamic markers — target transcript or protein knockdown — often carry the
exposure–response story better than any plasma metric.

## Disposition

| Area | What holds |
|---|---|
| Distribution | Rapid to liver and kidney; conjugation can direct hepatocyte uptake specifically |
| Metabolism | Nuclease-mediated, not CYP |
| Elimination | Renal excretion of the parent and shortened metabolites |
| Protein binding | High and chemistry-dependent; drives the distribution profile |
| Half-life | Plasma hours; tissue weeks to months |

## Interaction assessment

CYP and transporter liability is generally low, and the assessment is usually
model- and in-vitro-supported rather than clinical. Two things still deserve attention:
competition for the same uptake pathway when another agent targets the same receptor,
and any change in renal function that alters elimination.

State which mechanisms were assessed and found inapplicable. "No DDI expected" without
that list is an unsupported claim.

## Class effects that are not compound-specific

Several safety and tolerability signals track the chemistry class rather than the
target — injection-site reactions, complement activation, thrombocytopenia,
hepatotoxicity and renal effects appear across programmes with related backbones. The
informative comparator is often another oligonucleotide of the same chemistry, not
another drug against the same target.

## Populations

- **Renal impairment** is the population question for this modality, since renal
  elimination is a principal route.
- **Hepatic impairment** matters where the liver is both the target organ and a
  clearance route.
- **Immunogenicity** exists but behaves differently from protein therapeutics.

## Bioanalysis

Hybridisation-based or chromatographic assays with distinct validation expectations.
Distinguishing full-length parent from chain-shortened metabolites is a real analytical
question, and metabolite interference can inflate apparent parent exposure.

## What this context does not do

It does not select the exposure metric or accept a knockdown result as evidence of
efficacy. It states why the plasma curve is the least informative part of the picture.
