# Context — cell, gene and regenerative therapy

**Dimension:** modality · **Attaches after selection; never selected**

The modality where conventional pharmacokinetics stops applying and the vocabulary
itself has to change. A skill that reaches for AUC here is asking a question the product
cannot answer.

## The vocabulary changes

| Conventional | What replaces it |
|---|---|
| Absorption | Not applicable — administration is the delivery |
| Distribution | **Biodistribution** — where the vector or cells go, measured by tissue sampling or imaging |
| Metabolism | Not applicable |
| Excretion | **Shedding** — vector in body fluids, an environmental and contact question |
| Concentration–time profile | **Expansion, persistence, and contraction** for cells; **transduction and expression duration** for gene transfer |
| Half-life | Persistence, often measured in months or years |
| Steady state | Usually no such state; a single administration may be the entire treatment |
| Accumulation | Not applicable; **durability** is the question |

Reporting "PK could not be characterised" is wrong when the correct statement is that
kinetics is described by expansion and persistence, and those were measured.

## What exposure means here

- **Cell therapies:** peak expansion, time to peak, area under the expansion curve, and
  persistence duration — usually from transgene copies in blood. Expansion often
  correlates with both efficacy and cytokine-driven toxicity, so it is the closest thing
  to an exposure metric and it drives both sides.
- **Gene therapies:** vector genome copies in tissue, transgene expression level, and
  duration of expression. The measurable proximal endpoint is expression, not
  concentration.

Dose is expressed in cells or vector genomes, not mass, and the relationship between
administered dose and achieved expansion is weak — a critical difference from every
other modality.

## Immunogenicity is a gate, not a covariate

Pre-existing immunity to the vector can exclude a patient entirely, and post-treatment
immunity can preclude redosing. This makes immunogenicity an eligibility and
one-shot-durability question rather than a variability term.

## Populations and interactions

Conventional organ-impairment and DDI frameworks largely do not apply, and saying so
with the reason is the correct output. What replaces them: lymphodepletion regimen
effects on expansion, concomitant immunosuppression, prior therapy history, and
target-antigen burden.

## Safety pattern

Toxicities are modality-defining rather than incidental — cytokine release, neurologic
events, prolonged cytopenia, insertional risk, and durability failure. Several are
expansion-related, which ties them back to the exposure surrogate.

## Long-term follow-up

Multi-year follow-up is an expectation for integrating vectors and engineered cells. A
development plan without it is incomplete for reasons that have nothing to do with
efficacy.

## What this context does not do

It does not judge whether a persistence result is adequate, decide a cell dose, or
assess durability. It stops a skill from applying a framework that does not fit and
calling the result an absence of data.
