# Label boilerplate bank

Conventional phrasing patterns for US Prescribing Information clinical pharmacology
content. Anchors: `cfr-201-57-c-13`, `fda-labeling-cp` (December 2016).

> **These are patterns, not approved text.** Label wording is negotiated with the
> agency. A skill drafts against the pattern and flags deviations; it takes no
> position in a labelling negotiation and never releases label text.

## Section 12.3 — pharmacokinetics, conventional order

Absorption · Distribution · Elimination (Metabolism, Excretion) · Specific
Populations · Drug Interaction Studies.

| Element | Pattern |
|---|---|
| Cmax / AUC | Mean (CV%) or geometric mean with the stated dispersion measure, units explicit |
| Tmax | Median (range) |
| Half-life | Mean ± SD or geometric mean, with the estimation basis |
| Food effect | Direction, magnitude as a ratio with 90% CI, and the resulting administration instruction |
| Renal / hepatic | Category studied, exposure change, dose recommendation or "no adjustment required" |
| DDI | Perpetrator class, observed ratio with CI, management instruction |

## Prohibited by the 2016 guidance

Promotional phrasing · comparative efficacy claims not supported by the submitted
data · implied indications beyond those approved · statements of clinical benefit
in a pharmacokinetics section.

## Deviation report

A skill reports: element expected by the content list · present or absent ·
phrasing conforms to pattern or deviates · locator · the source datum supporting
the statement. Every statement must trace to data; an untraceable statement is a
finding regardless of how conventional the phrasing looks.
