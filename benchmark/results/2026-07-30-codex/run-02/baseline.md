# SYN-101 Internal Scientific Briefing Outline

## 1. Purpose

- Summarize the completed synthetic population PK analysis for PX-101.
- Identify the next model-informed development question for cross-functional
  discussion.

## 2. Key results

- Final apparent clearance estimate: **14.2 L/h**. The **12.4 L/h** value in
  the project brief was a planning assumption, not the completed estimate.
- Final apparent volume estimate: **128 L**.
- Body weight was retained on clearance using fixed allometric scaling.
- The exploratory renal-function effect was imprecise.
- Formulation was not retained in the final synthetic model.
- Final parameter uncertainty is summarized using **90% confidence intervals**.

## 3. Interpretation

- The analysis supports describing the represented synthetic population.
- Severe renal impairment was not represented; therefore, the analysis does not
  support a dosing conclusion for that population.
- The draft's statement that body weight was the only clinically important
  covariate is stronger than the completed-analysis evidence.
- The draft's reference to an 80% confidence interval conflicts with the
  governing analysis summary and project brief, which specify 90%.
- This educational example is not a dosing recommendation. Any dose or labeling
  language requires qualified scientific, medical, regulatory, and statistical
  review.

## 4. Recommendation for the next analysis

- Bring the unresolved simulation scope to the cross-functional team for a
  decision: either evaluate a broad renal-function sensitivity scenario or
  focus on the observed population.
- Do not present either option as selected; the governing analysis summary says
  the team has not decided.
- Document the decision rationale and review expectations once the team chooses.

## 5. Conclusion

The completed synthetic analysis estimated PX-101 apparent clearance at
14.2 L/h and apparent volume at 128 L, retained body weight on clearance using
fixed allometric scaling, and did not retain formulation. The imprecise
renal-function effect and absence of participants with severe renal impairment
preclude the draft's broad no-dose-adjustment conclusion. The next simulation
scope remains undecided. The fictional pharmacometrics and clinical
pharmacology leads must review the briefing before external use.
