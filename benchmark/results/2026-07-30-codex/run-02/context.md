# SYN-101 Internal Scientific Briefing Outline

## 1. Purpose

Prepare a fictional internal scientific briefing that summarizes the completed
PX-101 population PK analysis and identifies the next model-informed
development question. `[Project-Brief.md]`

## 2. Confirmed results

- Final apparent clearance was **14.2 L/h**.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- The **12.4 L/h** value was a planning assumption and is not the completed
  estimate. `[Project-Brief.md; Analysis-Summary.md]`
- Final apparent volume was **128 L**.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- Body weight was retained on clearance using fixed allometric scaling.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- The exploratory renal-function effect was imprecise.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- Participants with severe renal impairment were not represented.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- Formulation was not retained in the final synthetic model.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`
- Final parameter uncertainty is reported using **90% confidence intervals**.
  `[Analysis-Summary.md]`

## 3. Interpretation limited to represented evidence

The completed synthetic analysis supports describing PX-101 population PK in
the represented population. `[Analysis-Summary.md]` It does not support a
dosing conclusion for severe renal impairment because that population was not
represented and the exploratory renal-function effect was imprecise.
`[Analysis-Summary.md]`

The retained body-weight relationship should be reported as a model result, not
expanded into a claim that body weight was the only clinically important
covariate. `[Analysis-Summary.md; Draft-Conclusion.md]` This briefing is not a
dosing recommendation, and any proposed dose or labeling language requires
qualified review. `[Project-Brief.md; Analysis-Summary.md]`
