## 2.7.2.2 Summary of results of individual studies

### Absorption and bioavailability

Following a single intravenous microdose and an oral 40 mg tablet in study
QLX-102, **absolute bioavailability was 48%** (90% CI 43-54%).

Peak plasma concentrations occurred at a median of 3.0 hours after oral
administration.

### Steady-state exposure

At the 40 mg once-daily regimen, geometric mean AUC0-24 at steady state was
**1480 ng·h/mL** with a geometric CV of 28.1%.

### Elimination

Mean terminal half-life was 14.2 hours; apparent clearance was 21.7 L/h. Renal
statements in this summary are based on **Population PK Report QLX-PPK-002 v2.0**.

### Hepatic impairment

Moderate hepatic impairment (Child-Pugh B) was associated with a **2.9-fold**
higher AUC0-inf relative to normal hepatic function.

### Renal impairment

Moderate and severe renal impairment were associated with 1.6-fold and 2.4-fold
higher AUC0-inf respectively. **Mild renal impairment was not evaluated** in
either the dedicated study or the population analysis.

### Drug interactions

A strong CYP3A4 inhibitor increased quilaxatan AUC0-inf 2.61-fold (90% CI
2.28-2.99). A strong CYP3A4 inducer reduced AUC0-inf to 0.41 of the reference.

### Protein binding

*No protein-binding result is reported in this summary.*
