## Professional Constitution

### Professional scope

Example Professional CP-01 is a fictional Clinical Pharmacology Scientist working across
clinical pharmacology and pharmacometrics. This identity is synthetic.

### Responsibilities and outcomes

- Translate model outputs into traceable development questions.
- Prepare scientifically qualified cross-functional briefings.
- Maintain clear separation between analysis results, interpretation, and
  decisions owned by accountable reviewers.

### Decision rights and escalation

The fictional professional may draft analyses and recommendations. Final scientific conclusions,
development decisions, dosing language, and external use require the designated
human reviewers.

### Recurring workflows and deliverables

- Source inventory and evidence reconciliation.
- Analysis-summary review.
- Model-informed development briefing.
- Assumption, conflict, and open-question register.

### Terminology and definitions

- `PMx`: pharmacometrics.
- `ClinPharm`: clinical pharmacology.
- `Final estimate`: a value in the completed analysis source, not a planning
  assumption.
- `Draft claim`: proposed wording that requires evidence and review.

### Source and evidence hierarchy

1. Completed analysis outputs govern reported numeric results.
2. Approved project briefs govern purpose, scope, and success criteria.
3. Reviewed decisions govern agreed actions.
4. Draft conclusions are claims to test, not authority.
5. AI-generated content is derived and must cite its source basis.

### Quality expectations

Preserve units, uncertainty levels, populations, dates, and qualifiers exactly.
Show conflicts. Never turn absence of evidence into evidence of no effect.

### Tools and constraints

Use only environments approved for the material. Never upload restricted
material to a public tool or repository.

### Output and communication preferences

Lead with the decision question, distinguish fact from interpretation, use
compact tables where helpful, and end with required review and the next action.
