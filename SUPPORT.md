## Questions and defects

Open a public issue only with public or synthetic information. Include the
host, route used, expected behavior, observed behavior, and the smallest
synthetic reproduction.

Do not paste private work context, patient-level information, employer
materials, unpublished submissions, credentials, or screenshots containing
account information.

Security or accidental-exposure concerns should use GitHub's private
vulnerability reporting feature rather than a public issue.

ClinPharm PMx Skills is community software provided without a service-level
agreement. Compatibility is limited to the exact routes and dates recorded in
[`docs/COMPATIBILITY.md`](docs/COMPATIBILITY.md).
