# Privacy and responsible-use model

## Core rule

Use only information permitted in the current AI environment.

The existence of a skill, project, file-upload control, enterprise license, or
connector does not prove that a specific document is approved for use.

## Before adding context

Confirm:

1. the AI product and account being used;
2. the employer or project policy that applies;
3. the classification of the proposed material;
4. whether uploaded content may be retained or used by the provider;
5. whether external tools, agents, or connectors receive the content; and
6. who must review the resulting work.

If any material answer is unknown, use public/synthetic context or stop.

## Never place in public ClinPharm PMx Skills channels

Do not put restricted information in:

- this GitHub repository;
- issues, discussions, pull requests, commit messages, or Actions logs;
- public benchmark prompts or outputs;
- demonstration videos, screenshots, or carousel slides; or
- public AI services.

## Minimal context principle

The most useful context is not the largest context. Include only information
that changes how the AI should perform the intended work.

Prefer:

- roles instead of personal names;
- source references instead of copied bodies;
- project codes instead of proprietary asset names;
- abstract responsibilities instead of confidential strategy;
- synthetic examples instead of live data; and
- explicit unknowns instead of plausible guesses.

## Human accountability

The skill may help prepare scientific, operational, medical, regulatory, or
commercial work. It does not authorize or perform final decisions. A qualified
human must review the output according to the user's organization and
jurisdiction.

## Metadata

Public DOCX assets are scrubbed of common creator, last-modified-by, custom
property, and revision-session metadata before release. Metadata scrubbing does
not redact semantic content; public-safe content is still required.
